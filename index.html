The Residence

Built for the hour when the desert turns gold

Desert Residence was designed around a single moment: late afternoon, when the McDowell Mountains flush rose and the whole house — glass, water, stone — catches the same light. Deep overhangs keep the interiors cool and calm; every principal room faces the view.

It is a house that asks very little of you. Solar keeps the pool warm, the systems run themselves, and the casita gives guests a hotel of their own.
